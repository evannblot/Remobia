## Prompt final

Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: photographic hero image for a restrained premium French phone-repair website, Atelier Mobile in Laval.
Primary request: a single contemporary graphite smartphone, face-up, with a black switched-off screen and very fine discreet natural hairline cracks, lying diagonally on a pale ivory travertine stone surface. A small precision screwdriver and one tiny understated spare part are nearby, secondary and visually quiet.
Style/medium: refined editorial product photography, realistic materials, photographed rather than a glossy computer render.
Composition/framing: portrait format close to 4:5. The phone is the central main subject, complete and comfortably inside the frame. Clean airy arrangement, no clutter, slightly elevated top-down camera angle. Phone diagonally aligned from lower-left toward upper-right.
Lighting/mood: soft natural daylight, calm professional atmosphere, realistic delicate shadows, premium quiet craft.
Color palette: warm ivory #f5f3eb, graphite black, restrained deep green #183d36 detail only on the screwdriver handle if natural.
Materials/textures: subtle porous travertine, matte dark metal phone rim, realistic black glass screen. No artificial glitter, no dramatic neon light.
Constraints: no logo, no text, no watermark, no person, no hand, no store interior, no packaging, no extra phones. No exaggerated shattered glass and no unsafe sharp fragments. This is a standalone photographic asset, not a webpage or UI mockup.
